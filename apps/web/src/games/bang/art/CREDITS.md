The outlines are unchanged; the game only scales and colours them. A character has no portrait:
it is shown by an outline of what it is good at, and a weapon by the nearest gun in the set.

The sheriff's star in `icons.ts`, and everything else on the table (cards, posters, the wood),
is drawn in this folder's own code. The original game's illustrations are not used.

The titles are set in [Alfa Slab One](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Alfa+Slab+One) by JM
Solé, licensed under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org) and bundled
through `@fontsource/alfa-slab-one`.
