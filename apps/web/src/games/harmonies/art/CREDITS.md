The outlines are unchanged; the game only scales and colours them. A few animals have no outline
of their own in the set and borrow the nearest one: the otter is a capybara, the stork an ibis,
the kingfisher a hummingbird, the warbler a finch and the salamander a gecko.

Everything else on the table (tokens, stacks, animal cubes, boards) is drawn in this folder's
own code.
